Guided Learning Steps
1️⃣
Rising Air Cools: Warm, moist air rises from the ocean. As it expands, the air temperature drops.
2️⃣
Cool Air Makes Clouds: Vapour condenses into droplets (or freezes into snow). Ghost [E]s burst into Solid Red [E]s, warming the surrounding air!
3️⃣
Clouds Make Precipitation: Heat released into the surrounding air fuels powerful updrafts, towering the cloud and producing heavy rain/snow.
